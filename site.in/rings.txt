<h2>Support Ring Lattice</h2>
<p>The internal support structure for the printable Earth, printed as 16
separate pieces per hemisphere on a 220&nbsp;mm square bed and assembled
inside the globe. Three <strong>horizontal rings</strong> (one per latitude
band) carry tabs that seat in a socket cut into the centre of each tile's
underside. Twelve <strong>meridian arcs</strong> run from the equator to the
cap ring, each keyed into the rings it crosses by a tongue-and-groove joint.
A <strong>cap stem</strong> posts up to the pole, tabs into the polar cap
tile's socket, and ties to the cap ring with spokes. Both hemispheres are
shown assembled; the faint wireframe sphere is the globe's surface
(R&nbsp;=&nbsp;100&nbsp;mm). Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom.</p>
