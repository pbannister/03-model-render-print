<h2>Assembly Frame</h2>
<p>The internal assembly frame for the printable Earth: a central hub with
one strut per tile, each strut ending in a pad that sits on the tile's flat
bottom face. The frame aligns every tile at its exact spherical position
(the pads match each tile's bottom outline, so rotation is fixed too) and
stiffens the assembled globe. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom; the faint
wireframe sphere is the globe's surface (R = 100 mm).</p>
