<h2>Interior Support (new approach)</h2>
<p>The new interior support replaces the reinforcing ribs (dropped). It is
built in steps: (1) the interior of the hemisphere is filled with a single
solid; (2) a vertical (polar-axis) cylinder is subtracted from the solid —
80 mm diameter, just inside the 60°-latitude row's 100 mm diameter, so the
shell is 10 mm thick at the 60° row and thicker toward the equator;
(3) the support is shown here with all rows of tiles of the hemisphere.</p>
<p>The grey solid is the support (shown semi-transparent so you can see the
tiles pressing against it): the shell is 60 mm thick at the equator, ~47 mm
at the 30° row, and 10 mm at the 60° row; the 80 mm bore runs from the
equator face up to where it meets the sphere (lat 66.4°), closing at the
top. The tiles are the real relief tiles of the hemisphere (north: N00
0–30°N, N30 30–60°N, N90 cap; the south is the mirror image). Drag to
rotate, scroll to zoom; the rotation axis is the Earth's polar axis. The
faint wireframe sphere is the globe's surface (R = 100 mm).</p>
