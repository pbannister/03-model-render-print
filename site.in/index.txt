<h1>Model Render Print</h1>

<p>
Printable 3D models of the Earth rendered from the global elevation
model (GDEM) data on the shared <code>/datasets</code> volume — the
ETOPO 2022 global relief model maintained by the
<code>model-elevation-earth</code> project.
</p>

<h2>Status</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Status: active.</li>
  <li>Data source: ETOPO 2022 (land elevation + seafloor bathymetry),
      15-arcsecond GeoTIFF tiles on <code>/datasets/dataset-etopo-2022/</code>.</li>
  <li>Goal: render the Earth's surface as STL tile models that load into a
      slicer (OrcaSlicer, PrusaSlicer) and print on a 3D printer.</li>
  <li>Live dataset and tile state: see the dashboard.</li>
</ul>

<h2>How it works</h2>

<p>
The project reads the GDEM tiles from the shared datasets volume, divides
the globe into roughly equal-size tiles along lines of latitude and
longitude (single round tiles at the poles), and renders each tile as a
binary STL with a flat bottom that sits against the printer bed. The
approach is decided in <code>documents/05-stl-approach.md</code>.
</p>

<h2>Printing workflow</h2>

<ul>
  <li><code>make build</code> renders the tile set into
      <code>dataflow.out/stl/</code> with <code>manifest.json</code>.</li>
  <li><code>make check</code> validates every tile (watertight, flat bottom,
      expected bounds).</li>
  <li>Load one tile into your slicer; millimeters, z = 0 is the bed plane.</li>
  <li>Use <code>--tiles</code> or <code>--region</code> to render a subset.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Assembly plan</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Hemispheres are assembled independently, then joined at the equator;
      each starts from a reinforcing rib that seats the first row of tiles
      and prevents cumulative error. Interactive view:
      <a href="rib.html">rib.html</a>.</li>
  <li>Generated by <code>make rib</code> into
      <code>dataflow.out/rib/</code>.</li>
  <li>Superseded: the internal hub-and-strut frame
      (<a href="frame.html">frame.html</a>, <code>make frame</code>) was too
      hard to assemble and align and has been dropped.</li>
</ul>
